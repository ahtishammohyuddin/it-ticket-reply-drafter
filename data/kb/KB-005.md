# Email not syncing in Outlook
Category: email
1. Check the status bar at the bottom of Outlook. If it says Working Offline, open the Send/Receive tab and turn off Work Offline.
2. Check that webmail at https://mail.example.com shows the missing emails. If it does, the problem is the Outlook app, not the mailbox.
3. Close Outlook, then reopen it. If emails are still missing, restart the laptop.
4. If the mailbox is close to full, archive or delete large items. Mailboxes stop receiving at 50 GB.
5. On a phone, remove and re-add the work account in the mail app.
