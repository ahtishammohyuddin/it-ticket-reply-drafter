# Report a suspicious email or possible phishing
Category: security
1. Do not click links, open attachments or reply.
2. Use the Report Phishing button in Outlook, which sends a copy to the security team.
3. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, report it straight away and change your password from a different device (see KB-001).
4. If files on your laptop look encrypted or renamed, disconnect from the network by turning off Wi-Fi, and do not restart. Call the service desk.
These reports are treated as security incidents and handled by the security team first.
