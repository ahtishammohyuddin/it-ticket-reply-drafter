# VPN will not connect
Category: network_vpn
1. Check the internet works without VPN by opening any public website.
2. Restart the VPN client fully: right-click the tray icon, choose Quit, then open it again.
3. Make sure you are signing in with your work username and a current password. An expired password stops the VPN from connecting (see KB-001).
4. On hotel or public Wi-Fi, complete the Wi-Fi sign-in page first, then connect the VPN.
5. If the client shows error 809 or 812, restart the laptop and try a different network such as a phone hotspot.
If several people cannot connect at the same time, report it as a possible outage.
