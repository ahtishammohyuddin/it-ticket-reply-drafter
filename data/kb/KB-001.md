# Reset a forgotten password
Category: password_login
Use the self-service portal at https://passwordreset.example.com to reset your password.
1. Open the portal and enter your work username, not your email address.
2. Verify with the authenticator app or the backup phone number on your account.
3. Choose a new password: at least 12 characters, not used in the last 5 changes.
4. Wait 2 to 3 minutes, then sign in again. On a laptop, connect to the office network or VPN so the new password syncs.
The service desk will never ask for your password. If the portal says your account is locked, follow KB-002.
