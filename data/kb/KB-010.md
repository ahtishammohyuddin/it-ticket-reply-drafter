# Access to a system, app or shared folder
Category: access_request
1. Access is granted on your line manager's approval. Ask your manager to approve, or include their name in your request.
2. Say exactly which system, folder or app you need, and the level of access, for example read only or edit.
3. For a shared folder, include the full folder path.
4. Approved access usually works after you sign out and sign in again.
The service desk cannot grant access without approval from the owner of the system or folder.
