# Laptop running slowly
Category: hardware
1. Restart the laptop. Many slowdowns clear after a restart, especially if it has not been restarted in over a week.
2. Install pending updates from Software Center, then restart again.
3. Open Task Manager and check whether one app is using most of the CPU or memory. Close it if it is not needed.
4. Keep at least 15% of the disk free. Move large personal files to your cloud drive.
5. If the laptop is over 4 years old or still slow after these steps, the service desk can arrange a hardware check.
